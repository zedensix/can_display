Never 12V on the PicoOnly through the 12V to 5V converter, its micro-USB plug in the Pico's socket. Take a switched 12V (ACC/ignition fuse tap), not OBD pin 16: it stays live with the car off.
3.3V logic onlyThe encoder's + goes to 3V3(OUT), not 5V: its pull-ups would put 5V on the RP2040, which isn't 5V tolerant.
Keep GP15 freeThe pico-sdk borrows it at boot (RP2040-E5). GPIO23, 24, 25 and 29 belong to the wireless chip.
OBD dongle: no wireThe Vgate iCar Pro 2S plugs into the OBD port; the Pico W talks to it over BLE. One client at a time: a phone connected to it hides it.
MCP2515 (wired CAN)VCC on VBUS (5V). Its SO swings to 5V: level shift it before GP12. Remove jumper J1 (120Ω termination). 8MHz crystal, 500kbps.
Flashing in the carThe Pico has one USB socket: unplug the converter's micro-USB, plug the Mac's cable instead.
