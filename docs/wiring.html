Never 12V on the PicoOnly through the 12V to 5V buck, into VSYS. Take a switched 12V (ACC/ignition fuse tap), not OBD pin 16: it stays live with the car off.
3.3V logic onlyThe encoder's + goes to 3V3(OUT), not 5V: its pull-ups would put 5V on the RP2040, which isn't 5V tolerant.
Keep GP15 freeThe pico-sdk borrows it at boot (RP2040-E5). GPIO23, 24, 25 and 29 belong to the wireless chip.
OBD dongle: no wireThe Vgate iCar Pro 2S plugs into the OBD port; the Pico W talks to it over BLE. One client at a time: a phone connected to it hides it.
MCP2515 (optional, wired CAN)Its SO swings to 5V: level shift it before GP12. Remove jumper J1 (120Ω termination). 8MHz crystal, 500kbps.
USB and VSYS togetherTo flash or read logs with the buck feeding VSYS, put a Schottky diode between buck OUT+ and VSYS, or unplug one supply.
